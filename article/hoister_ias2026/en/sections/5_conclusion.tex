\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This draft formulates the hoisting overspeed monitoring problem as state-graph-constrained progression learning rather than flat multiclass classification. The proposed SGPH-Net combines current-state recognition, short-horizon worsening prediction, and time-to-fault bucket estimation in a single compact framework that is aligned with the operational semantics of the dataset. The main value of the method is the explicit use of a directed state graph to encode legal degradation and recovery transitions.

The current limitation is straightforward: the full benchmark has not yet been inserted. Before submission, the paper must be completed with file-level comparison results, warning metrics, and ablations that verify the contribution of graph constraint and hazard modeling. Once these experiments are finished, the draft can be tightened into an IEEE IAS conference submission and, if the empirical signal is strong enough, later extended toward a TIA manuscript.
